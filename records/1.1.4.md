## Qualification

45 focused unit checks across seven HAL, capture ownership, microphone, watchdog,
speech input and diagnostic suites passed. These supplement the developer-recorded
unit/live-capture results in the supplied commits; the full suites were not rerun.
36 offline packaging cases, 24 publishing fixtures, 179 local documentation
links/anchors and whitespace validation passed.

The automated live-meter UI runner failed before connecting to the app; the retry
could not locate its generated runner. This is not a passing UI test. Native signed
app checks supplied direct lifecycle evidence instead.

The candidate workflow passed archive/export, identity continuity, runtime and
entitlements, notarization, stapling, Gatekeeper, extracted ZIP, checksum and Sparkle
signature verification. The exact notarized ZIP was launched temporarily on the
owner's macOS 26.7.1/M5 Pro Mac. Live metering received frames, stopped/restarted
across navigation, and stopped on Settings closure and resumed on reopening.
No render failures, oversized callbacks or overflows appeared in the checked events.

Two Apple Speech setup trials reached recording with positive raw/input/converted/
submitted PCM counts, then completed cancellation and native retirement. Setup
suppressed History, cleanup and automatic text delivery; no audio or transcript
content was inspected or exported. Apple asset installation was requested during
preparation. No Parakeet download or permission grant/reset was performed. The
installed app bundle and selected speech-model preference were preserved.

## Limits

The affected M3/virtual-output case has not been requalified. This release targets
that failure path without claiming a confirmed remote resolution. Parakeet V2/V3
were shown as Download required in release storage and were not tested in this
signed package. Spoken transcript correctness, forced ring overflow, full device/
appearance coverage, macOS 26.0 runtime and a clean-account end-to-end Sparkle
installation remain unverified. Off-device signing-key backups remain unverified;
no key was exported or changed.

## <a id="speech-models"></a>Speech models

- Choose Apple Speech, Parakeet TDT 0.6B V2 for English, or Parakeet V3 with automatic recognition across 25 European languages.
- Download Parakeet models from Settings → Models, then choose an installed model from Models or Dictation. Apple Speech remains the default.
- See live local previews while speaking. Parakeet performs a final pass before cleanup and output; brief recordings may finish before a preview appears.
- Follow download progress through downloading, verifying, and installing. Downloads continue while navigating or closing Settings with the app open. Quitting stops unfinished downloads; start the download again after reopening.
- Inspect searchable languages, recognition behavior, source/license information, and model storage through compact cards and their info buttons.

## <a id="model-comparison"></a>Model comparison

**Compare models** opens a reusable window with 22 speech entries, including Apple Speech, and six cleanup options. Choose up to three entries in an aligned comparison deck, swap models, show differences, or filter/search the catalog by language, availability, and capabilities.

Inspect reference speed, accuracy, download size, language coverage, and capability details. Handy reference values identify their snapshot and scoring limitations. Apple Speech remains **Not rated**; cleanup speed and fidelity remain **Not measured**. Research-only entries provide information; model installation and selection stay in Settings → Models.

The window adapts its details into an inspector or sheet as it resizes. **Return to Models** brings Settings forward while preserving the comparison. Keyboard navigation, descriptive metric labels, and stronger borders/text under Increase Contrast support browsing.

## <a id="settings-and-history"></a>Settings and History

Settings now follows **Overview → Dictation → Models → Audio → Vocabulary → History → General → About**, including section shortcuts and cycling.

History records which speech model produced a transcript. Retry with a chosen installed model, compare transcript versions, and keep the preferred result. Existing recordings use Apple Speech as their original model. A recording already underway keeps the model it started with.

## <a id="requirements-and-limits"></a>Requirements and limits

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 26.0 or later. Transcription and optional cleanup run locally; model downloads require a connection. Completed Parakeet downloads work offline. History remains opt-in, and automatic update checks remain off by default.

Parakeet V3's language list excludes Chinese and Malay. Apple speech assets and Apple Intelligence cleanup availability depend on macOS and language support. Research ratings describe external reference artifacts and configurations; WhisperOrigo performance ratings await app-specific benchmarks. The research catalog ships locally and does not trigger model downloads.
